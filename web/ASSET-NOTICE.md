The public website uses reviewed images produced by unchanged RikUI addon source in the Lua fixture renderer. Each capture records its original client, renderer, fixture, asset and addon provenance. Representative names, items and activity states are fixture data. The component editor composes these actual images with validated geometry; it does not recreate game widgets in HTML or SVG.

Client executables, extracted archives, Blizzard source trees, raw client tables and private player records remain local. The former independent mock scenes and their unused extracted icon/map assets have been retired. Historical provenance remains in repository history.

Existing whole-world documentation plates retain their reviewed screenshot provenance under tools/site-renders/worlds. New captures require current verified client inputs; historical plates are not evidence of a current client build.
