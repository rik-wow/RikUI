The public website uses reviewed images produced by unchanged RikUI addon source in the Lua fixture renderer. Each capture records its original client, renderer, fixture, asset and addon provenance. Representative names, items and activity states are fixture data. The component editor composes these actual images with validated geometry; it does not recreate game widgets in HTML or SVG.

Only rendered images and RikUI's public configuration-engine source are distributed. Client executables, extracted archives, Blizzard source trees, raw client tables and private player records remain local. The former independent mock scenes and their unused extracted icon/map assets have been retired. Historical provenance remains in repository history.

Studio's 3840 × 2160 background is the exact user-supplied game screenshot, published at the user's request. Its original character and overhead names remain part of the fixed image shared by every preview. Only the original JPEG and its reviewed hash and filename provenance are shipped; this historical screenshot is not evidence of the current client build. Lossless cropped component images retain the source capture geometry and are verified pixel-for-pixel against their reviewed Lua captures.

Existing whole-world documentation plates retain their reviewed screenshot provenance under tools/site-renders/worlds. New captures require current verified client inputs; historical plates are not evidence of a current client build.
