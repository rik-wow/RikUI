# Buffs and debuffs

## Player buffs and debuffs

Buffs and debuffs appear near the top right of the screen. Each icon has a duration swipe and, when relevant, a stack count.

![Player buffs](render:auras-buffs)

Debuff borders show their type: blue for magic, purple for curses, brown for diseases, green for poison and red for other effects.

![Player debuffs](render:auras-debuffs)

Temporary weapon enchants appear with your buffs, using the weapon icon and remaining charges.

Hover an icon for its tooltip. Right-click a removable player buff or weapon enchant to cancel it.

## Target, pet and focus

Aura rows sit beside their unit frames. Your own effects are larger and brighter; effects from other casters are smaller and dimmed.

![Target auras](render:auras-target) ![Pet auras](render:auras-pet) ![Focus auras](render:auras-focus)

A warm border may pulse when an effect is ready to refresh.

## Choose the rows you want

The **Auras** module controls player buffs, debuffs and enchants. **Unit auras** controls the rows attached to unit frames. Change them under **Settings → System → Modules**, then reload.
