# Objective widgets

## Objectives and activity displays

Objective widgets show information for your current activity. They can include progress bars, two opposing bars, capture progress, resources, spell icons and short instructions.

![Status bar](render:widget-status) ![Double status bar](render:widget-double)

RikUI gives text and bars a consistent appearance while keeping useful icons and objective artwork.

![Icon and text](render:widget-icon)

These displays appear when an activity provides them. Their values and completion conditions come from the game.
