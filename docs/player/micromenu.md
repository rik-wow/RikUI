# Micro menu and bag strip

## Game menus

The compact micro menu opens the available game panels, such as your character, spellbook and social windows. Hover a button to see its name.

![Micro menu](render:micro-menu)

Buttons appear according to what your client makes available.

## Bag strip

The backpack and equipped-bag buttons show their icons and free space. Empty bag slots have their own buttons, and a keyring button appears when your client provides one.

![Backpack](render:micro-bags)

Click to open your inventory. When RikUI's bag module is enabled, bag buttons open the combined [inventory window](bags.md).

Use `/rik move` to arrange the menu and bag groups.
