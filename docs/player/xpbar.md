# Experience and reputation

## Experience

The experience bar shows your progress toward the next level. Rested experience has a separate fill so you can see the bonus remaining.

![Experience](preview:xp-experience) ![Rested experience](render:xp-rested)

Right-click to switch to the compact 8-pixel bar, or move the control under the picture to compare the two. Use the settings to choose labels and whether gains briefly appear above it.

![Experience gain](render:xp-gain)

## Reputation

When you watch a faction, its reputation appears in a separate bar. Left-click that bar outside combat to open the reputation panel.

![Watched reputation](render:xp-reputation)

Use `/rik move` to position the bars alongside your action bars or elsewhere on screen.
