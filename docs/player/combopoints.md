# Combo points

## Combo points

Five small pips show your available combo points. Empty pips stay dark; filled pips light up as you build points.

![Empty points](render:combo-empty) ![Three points](render:combo-three) ![Full points](render:combo-full)

The display is available for supported rogue and druid combo-point displays. Use `/rik move` to position it near your target or resource bar.

Turn **Combo points** on or off under **Settings → System → Modules**, then reload.
