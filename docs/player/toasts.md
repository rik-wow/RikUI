# Social notifications

## Social notices

Friend, play-time and voice notices appear with RikUI's dark backgrounds and flat borders. Their text, buttons and duration follow the game.

![Friend notification](render:toast-friend) ![Play-time notice](render:toast-time) ![Voice notice](render:toast-voice)

Use the game's social and notification settings to choose which notices you receive.

Disable **Toasts** under **Settings → System → Modules** and reload to restore the usual styling.
