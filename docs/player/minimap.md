# Minimap

## The minimap

The square minimap shows your current area, with the zone name above it and the clock and coordinates below.

![Square minimap](render:minimap-square)

Tracking, mail, queue indicators and map pings remain available. New mail shows an envelope in the top corner; the group-finder eye sits in the bottom corner. Right-click the map for the tracking menu to choose what you want to find nearby.

![Mail and queue indicators](render:minimap-indicators)

## RikUI menu

The **RikUI** button opens settings, frame movement, setup and other tools. While frames are unlocked, it changes to **Done**.

Use `/rik move` to reposition the minimap group. **Settings → Minimap** adds a performance readout with your framerate and world latency, a day or night marker, and server time in place of local time.

![Performance readout](render:minimap-performance)
