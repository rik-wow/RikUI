# Small dialogs

## Small windows

Ready checks, stack splitting, colour selection, friend requests and other small dialogs use the same borders and controls as RikUI's larger windows.

![Ready check](render:dlg-readycheck) ![Role poll](render:dlg-rolepoll) ![Stack split](render:dlg-stacksplit)

- **Ready check:** choose ready or not ready.
- **Role poll:** pick the role you will play.
- **Stack split:** choose the number of items, then confirm.
- **Queue ready:** accept or leave the queue when prompted.
- **Colour picker:** choose a colour and accept or cancel it.
- **Add friend:** enter the character or Battle.net name and submit.

![Queue ready](render:dlg-queueready) ![Colour picker](render:dlg-colorpicker) ![Add friend](render:dlg-addfriend)

Report forms, name autocompletion and the older dropdown lists use the same styling.

![Report](render:dlg-report) ![Autocomplete](render:dlg-autocomplete)

Available dialogs depend on the activity and your client. Closing or cancelling a dialog keeps the game's usual behaviour.
