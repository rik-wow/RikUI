# Loot and rolls

## Loot an item

Click an item row to loot it. Each row shows the icon, quality-coloured name and stack count, and the title counts the items left. Coins appear together on a money row.

![Loot list](render:loot-list) ![Currency row](render:loot-coins)

Hover for a tooltip. Modified clicks still let you link an item or preview equipment. Escape closes the loot window.

## Choose where loot opens

By default, the list opens at your cursor. Turn off **Open the loot list at the cursor** in Settings to give it a fixed position, then use `/rik move` to place it.

The list shrinks as items are looted. Your game's auto-loot setting continues to control automatic looting.

## Group rolls

Need, Greed and Pass keep their usual behaviour. Roll windows use the same flat borders and quality colours as the loot list.

![Need and greed roll](render:loot-roll)

Bind-on-pickup and other item confirmations still appear when required.

![Loot confirmation](render:loot-confirm)
