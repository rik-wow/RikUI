# Durability

## Worn and broken gear

The durability indicator appears when gear needs attention. Worn pieces are listed in yellow; broken pieces appear in red. Hover for a list of affected equipment and its durability.

![Worn gear](render:dur-worn) ![Broken gear](render:dur-broken) ![Gear tooltip](render:dur-tooltip)

Once repaired, the warning disappears.

## Keep durability visible

Enable **Show lowest durability** to see your lowest equipment durability before a warning appears. Worn and broken warnings take priority when needed.

![Lowest durability](render:dur-lowest)

Use `/rik move` to position the indicator.
