# Class effects

## Follow your class effects

The class-effect display gives tracked buffs and target effects their own space near the combat HUD. Player effects and target effects are separate movable groups.

![Player effects](render:effects-player) ![Target effects](render:effects-target)

Icons show the spell, its duration and stacks when available. An effect disappears when it ends or no longer applies to your target.

## Arrange the display

Use `/rik move` to place the two groups. Turn **Class auras** on or off under **Settings → System → Modules**, then reload.

The tracked effects depend on your class and learned abilities. Effects outside that list still appear in the normal [buff and debuff rows](auras.md).
