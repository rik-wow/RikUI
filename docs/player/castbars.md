# Cast bars

## Reading a cast bar

Each bar shows the spell icon, spell name and cast progress. The remaining time appears on the right.

- **Gold** fills as a spell is cast.
- **Blue** drains during a channel.
- **Red** marks a failed or interrupted cast.
- A brief green flash marks a completed cast.

![Interrupted cast](render:cast-interrupted) ![Channel](render:cast-channel)

The target and focus bars show a shield on spells that cannot be interrupted.

## Player, target, focus and pet

Player and target cast bars sit beneath their unit frames. The pet has a smaller bar. The focus bar sits above the focus frame to leave space for the pet.

![Target cast](render:cast-target) ![Focus cast](render:cast-focus) ![Pet cast](render:cast-pet)

Use `/rik move` to reposition each one.

## Change the size

Open **Settings → Castbars**. Width ranges from 75% to 150%, and height from 16 to 36 pixels. Reload after changing the size.

**Show remaining cast time** takes effect immediately. Turning it off hides the number while the progress bar keeps running.
