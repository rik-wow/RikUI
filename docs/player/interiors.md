# Window interiors

## Character and abilities

Equipment slots, character stats, reputation, skills, spellbook and talents use matching rows and icon borders. Open these panels with your normal menu buttons or keys.

![Character equipment](render:int-character) ![Character stats](render:int-stats) ![Reputation and skills](render:int-reputation)

![Spellbook](render:int-spellbook)

Tooltips, equipment comparisons and spell buttons work as usual.

## Quests and conversations

Quest dialogue and gossip windows keep the NPC's text and choices. Objective and reward sections are separated so you can review them before accepting or completing a quest.

![Quest dialogue](render:int-quest) ![Gossip choices](render:int-gossip)

## Buying, crafting and storage

Merchant, bank, mail, trade, trainer and profession windows use the same style. Item icons retain quality borders and stack counts.

![Merchant](render:int-merchant) ![Trade](render:int-trade)

The inbox and an opened letter have different controls. Trading still requires both players to accept. Buying, repairs and crafting follow the game's prices and requirements.

![Mail inbox](render:int-inbox)

See [Auction house](auction-house.md) for browsing, bidding and selling.

## Social and group panels

Friends, guild, group-finder, raid-information and PvP panels keep their lists and controls. Inspect windows show the selected player's equipment.

![Friends](render:int-friends) ![Guild and communities](render:int-communities) ![Raid information](render:int-raidinfo)

![Inspect](render:int-inspect)

## Other game panels

Available calendar, achievement, collection, stable and other activity panels receive matching styling. A panel appears only when that feature is available in your client.

![Calendar](render:int-calendar) ![Achievements](render:int-achievements) ![Collections](render:int-collections)
