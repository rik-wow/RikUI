# Action bars

## Arrange your bars

RikUI provides three action rows and two side columns, with separate stance and pet controls. Use `/rik move` to place each group.

![Main action row](render:bars-main)

The second and third rows stack above the main row. The third row fades while idle and shows again on hover, in combat, or while you move it.

![Secondary action row](render:bars-secondary) ![Third action row](render:bars-third)

The two side columns hold the same kind of buttons in a vertical arrangement.

![Right action column](render:bars-right) ![Left action column](render:bars-left)

Your normal action-bar keys and buttons still work. Drag spells and items onto buttons outside combat.

## Labels and empty slots

Open **Settings → Bars and layout** to show or hide empty slots, key labels and cooldown numbers, and to fade any bar until you hover it. Empty slots appear while bars are unlocked so you can see the full row.

Ghost icons mark preset abilities you have not learned yet, with the level that teaches them. Use `/rik ghosts on` or `/rik ghosts off` to change them. A ghost icon cannot cast the spell.

![Empty preset slot](render:bars-ghost)

## Pages, stances and pets

Changing your action page or stance changes the corresponding main-bar buttons. A manually selected page shows that page's twelve actions in place of the main row. A form with its own actions, such as a druid's Bear Form, does the same.

![Manual page overlay](render:bars-page) ![Stance page overlay](render:bars-stance-page)

Stance controls show learned forms and stances. Pet actions appear when you have a pet. Right-click autocast toggling is not available on RikUI's pet buttons.

![Stance buttons](render:bars-stance) ![Pet actions](render:bars-pet)

## Other bar options

`/rik gryphons on` and `/rik gryphons off` control the end artwork. `/rik stockbars show` and `/rik stockbars hide` control the stock bars.

Use [Import and export](sharing.md) to share a preset, and [Move frames](layout.md) to save a different arrangement.
