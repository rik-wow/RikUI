# Weapon timers

## Weapon swings

Weapon timers show the current main-hand, off-hand and ranged weapon cycles. Each bar fills over the swing and shows the seconds left; dual-wielding shows two different swing times.

![Main hand](render:swing-main-hand) ![Off hand](render:swing-off-hand) ![Ranged cycle](render:swing-ranged)

The timers sit at the bottom of the combat column. Use `/rik move` to arrange them elsewhere.

## Ranged movement cues

The ranged timer can show a movement window and a cue to stop for the next shot. Treat these as timing help: moving, changing targets and interrupted shots can change what happens next.

![Move window](render:swing-move) ![Stop window](render:swing-stop)

An unknown range is shown as unknown. The timer cannot guarantee that a shot is in range.

![Unknown range](render:swing-unknown)
