# Alert toasts

## Loot and reward alerts

Loot, money, recipes and other available reward alerts appear in compact dark panels. The icon sits beside the reward text, with a gold accent at the edge.

![Loot alert](render:alert-loot) ![Money alert](render:alert-money) ![Achievement alert](render:alert-achievement)

Hover or click an alert where the game offers more information. Alerts disappear on their usual timers.

Turn off **Alerts** under **Settings → System → Modules** and reload to restore their original appearance.
