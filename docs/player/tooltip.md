# Tooltips

## Unit, item and spell information

Tooltips use dark backgrounds, thin borders and the same font as the rest of RikUI. Item comparisons keep separate panels beside the main tooltip.

![Item tooltip](render:tip-item) ![Aura tooltip](render:tip-aura)

Item levels and spell IDs appear where available. Aura tooltips show the effect you are hovering. Unit tooltips carry a health bar under the text.

## Size and position

Open **Settings → Tooltips** to adjust tooltip scale from 75% to 150%. Move the control under the picture to compare sizes.

![Unit tooltip](preview:tip-unit)

Use `/rik move` to place the tooltip anchor. Tooltips attached to specific controls, such as the help text in Settings, still appear beside those controls.
