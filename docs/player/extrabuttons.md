# Extra action buttons

## Special actions

Quest items, zone abilities, spell flyouts, possess actions and vehicle controls keep their usual buttons and key behaviour, with RikUI's borders and labels.

![Zone ability](render:extra-zone)

These controls appear only when your current activity provides them. Spell flyouts open from their action-bar button.

Use `/rik move` for the special-action groups that have a mover. Changes to protected controls must be made outside combat.
