# Quest tracker

## Your watched quests

The tracker lists watched quests with their objectives underneath. Completed quests show a turn-in message; failed quests are marked so you can distinguish them from unfinished objectives.

![Active objective](render:tracker-active) ![Completed quest](render:tracker-complete) ![Failed quest](render:tracker-failed)

Click the header to collapse the list to its title and count, and click it again to open it.

![Tracker header](render:tracker-header)

If the list runs out of room, or **Maximum visible quests** caps it under **Settings → Quest tracker**, an overflow count shows how many more quests are watched. Open the quest log to see the rest.

![Overflow count](render:tracker-overflow)

## Position and guidance

Use `/rik move` to place the tracker. The [quest planner](questplanner.md) adds a guidance row above the list, and [timed quests](questtimers.md) appear above the tracker.
