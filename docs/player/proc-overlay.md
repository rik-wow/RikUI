# Proc indicators

## Ability cues

Compact gold lines near your character signal ability activations reported by the game. Cues can appear at the sides, above, below or in the centre.

![Left and right cues](render:proc-sides) ![Upper and lower cues](render:proc-vertical) ![Central cue](render:proc-center)

Which cues appear depends on your class and the effects the game provides. The display does not add alerts for every buff.

Disable **Proc overlay** under **Settings → System → Modules** and reload if you prefer the original artwork.
