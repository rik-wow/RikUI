# Small HUD elements

## Small on-screen labels

RikUI applies its font to queue information, the framerate label and quest-navigation labels.

![Framerate label](render:hud-framerate) ![Quest navigation marker](render:hud-navigation)

Their content and timing follow the game. Queue information appears while you are queued or listed; quest markers appear for available navigation.

![Queue status](render:hud-queue)

Disable **HUD frames** under **Settings → System → Modules** and reload to restore the usual styling.
